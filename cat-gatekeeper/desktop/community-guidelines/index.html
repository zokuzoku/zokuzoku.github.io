Round 1: We want to see everyone's cats!

July 5, 2026 10:00 – July 6, 2026 10:00 (JST)
Held on X (@konekone2026)

What we are looking for

We are looking for photos of your cat, or cat photos you took yourself.
Send us the best shots you cannot help but share, from cute moments to funny ones!

Selected photos will be distributed randomly to everyone's desktops through the Cat Gatekeeper app.
Let the world see your pride-and-joy cat!

Photo requirements

Please submit only photos for which you own the copyright.

How to enter

Entries will open occasionally on the app creator's X account (@konekone2026).
Reply to the entry post with your cat photo, your cat's name, country, and the social media account you would like displayed as credit. If no account is specified, the X account used to enter will be displayed.

Notes

  • Photos that involve third-party rights are not allowed.
  • Illustrations, drawings, and images of cats that do not exist are not eligible.
  • Photos may be cropped when published.
  • Photos that include people may be submitted only if everyone shown has given permission for publication.
  • Please submit only once per event.
  • Selected photos may be used in the app and may also be featured on social media.

Selected photos

  • About 30 photos are planned to be selected for each event.
  • Entries may close early if there are many submissions.
  • Selection is not first-come, first-served.
  • The creator will choose photos while considering overall balance and other factors.
  • Selected photos will be randomly delivered to everyone's desktops. They may be rotated over time, so they are not guaranteed to appear all the time.
  • Selected photos will display the country, cat name, and account name as credit.
