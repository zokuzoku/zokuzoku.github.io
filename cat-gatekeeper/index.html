Q&A

Which sites does it work on?

You can choose from X, Threads, Reddit, Facebook, YouTube, and Bluesky. (version 1.0)

Is it free?

Yes. Cat Gatekeeper is free to use.

Can I add custom sites?

This feature is planned for a future update.

I was asked to grant permissions.

These permissions are needed to show the cat on target sites. Cat Gatekeeper does not collect, sell, or transmit your browsing history or page content. The source code is open on GitHub.

Can I change the timer?

Yes. You can change it from the extension menu.

The cat does not appear.

Switching tabs resets the timer. First, set the timer to 1 minute and try again. If the cat still does not appear, it may be conflicting with another extension. Turn off other extensions and test it again.

Where can I open the menu?

You can open it from the extension icon near the address bar in the top-right of your browser.

Can I dismiss the cat?

Yes. Open the extension menu and press the dismiss button.

Which browsers are supported?

Cat Gatekeeper currently supports Chrome. Firefox support is planned, and other browsers are also under consideration.

Will new cats be added?

Yes. More cats are planned for future updates.
