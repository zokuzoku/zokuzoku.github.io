Q&A

Is this the same as the browser extension?

No. The browser extension is for social media sites in your browser. The desktop app is a timer-style app for your computer, and it also works for tasks outside the browser.

Which platforms are supported?

It works on macOS and Windows.

Can I change the timer length?

Yes. You can change both the time before the cat appears and the break time from the app menu.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The basic timer runs on your device.

Can I dismiss the cat?

Yes. You can dismiss it from the app menu, or by pressing the dismiss button that appears in the bottom-right corner of the screen when the cat appears.

Are the cats real?

They are ideal cats created one by one with the help of AI, carefully adjusted to become the ultimate lovable distraction. They are not real cats, but I’m very confident in their cuteness. Please treat them kindly as little gatekeepers for your desk.

Can I use the assets in the app?

You are very welcome to share screenshots or posts about Cat Gatekeeper. Please do not use or redistribute the image or video assets themselves.

Will new cats be added?

Yes. More cats are planned for future updates.
