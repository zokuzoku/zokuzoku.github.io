How It Works

When time is up, the cat appears.
It takes over your screen and makes you take a break.

Switching to another tab resets the timer.

01

Choose social sites

Select the social sites you want to limit.

02

Set the time

Set when the cat appears
and how long it stays.
Default: 60 minutes of use, 5 minutes of break.

03

Take a break

The cat takes over the page
until the countdown ends
You can also open the menu and dismiss it.
